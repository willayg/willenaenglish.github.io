AI Coach

Coach / intelligence / conversation without looking like a generic chatbot logo.

1 · Coach HeadsetFriendly, direct, obviously “coach.” Closest to the mark you already liked.
small-size check
2 · Smart SpeechConversation first. The pink signal dot makes it feel active rather than just “chat.”
3 · Learning RadarBest if Coach is positioned as “noticing” patterns and deciding what matters next.
4 · Coach CoreMore “AI system” than character. Strong on a control-room style surface.
5 · Pure CoachNo robot face. Headset + signal only. More mature and less “kids app.”
6 · Coach NoteBrief + chat combined in one mark. Fits the UI direction you’re leaning toward.

Book Study

Reference, learning, explanation, and “open this to understand it.”

1 · Open BookThe clearest and safest study mark.
2 · Study PageFeels like lesson/reference material rather than a generic book.
3 · Learn + SparkMore energetic. Good if Study is meant to feel exploratory.
4 · InspectStudy as “look closely / understand.” Distinct from Practice.
5 · Guided NotesStudy material with a small “understood” cue.
6 · Study DeskFeels like a focused workspace rather than a book icon.

Book Practice

Action, answering, checking, repetition, and measurable progress.

1 · Practice ChecksDirect and readable at small sizes.
2 · Target PracticeVery different from Study. Strong action language.
3 · WorkbookFeels academic but still active.
4 · ImprovePractice as visible progress rather than “test.”
5 · Answer + CheckGood if Practice is mostly question solving.
6 · Skill DrillCompact, game-like, and easy to distinguish from the Study book marks.
